ABOUT THE ACADEMY

Preparation built on purpose.

CDA Parasnath aims to provide a disciplined environment where aspirants can strengthen academic knowledge, develop effective study habits, and prepare systematically for competitive examinations.

Our approach brings together classroom learning, practice, revision, doubt-clearing, and fitness awareness. Course schedules, facilities, and services should be confirmed directly with the academy before admission.
